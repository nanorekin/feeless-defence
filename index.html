How to play

Goal

Enemies walk the path from the top to the ledger at the bottom. Each one that gets through costs Integrity. At 0 the ledger is breached and the run ends. Survive as many waves as you can.

Building

Pick a tower in the bar under the map, then tap empty tiles to build as many as you can afford. Tap the same tower again to stop building. Tap a tower on the map to see its range and to upgrade it (up to level 4) or sell it for 60% of what you spent. Selling asks you to confirm. Tiles between two lanes are the best spots because they cover both. Towers cannot go on the path.

Towers

Node: fast and cheap. Rep: long range, hits hard, slow. Pruner: splash damage, great against swarms. Throttle: slows enemies so everything else hits more often.

Enemies

Dust comes in swarms. Spam is the all-rounder. Bots are slow and tough and cost 2 Integrity. Every 5th wave an Audit boss arrives and costs 8 Integrity if it gets through.

Raw

Raw is the in-game money you earn from kills and from clearing waves. It has nothing to do with real XNO. Money is tight, so choose between a new tower and an upgrade.

Daily

The map and the twist change at 00:00 UTC and are the same for everyone. Reach wave 5 to keep your daily streak. You can retry as often as you like and your best wave for the day is kept.

Pause

Pause opens a menu where you can resume or restart the map. The game also pauses when you switch away from the page.

Keyboard

Arrow keys move the cursor, 1 to 4 choose a tower, Enter builds, U upgrades, S sells (then Y to confirm), N starts the wave, P pauses, Esc cancels.
